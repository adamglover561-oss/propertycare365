# Privacy / Data Safety Notes

Use these notes when completing Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data Safety forms. Confirm the final answers against the production build before submission.

## Data used by the service
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number (optional)
- Property address and access notes
- Account identifier
- Heating fuel / property service information
- Booking and job information
- Service reports
- Billing and invoice metadata
- Subscription status

## Data not stored by Property Care 365
Full payment-card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored in the Property Care 365 application database.

## Purposes
- Account management
- Providing plumbing and heating services
- Booking and appointment management
- Customer support
- Billing and subscription administration
- Service reports and maintenance records
- Security, fraud prevention and legal compliance

## Third-party processors / integrations
- Supabase: authentication, database and storage
- Stripe: payments, subscriptions and invoices
- Google Calendar: appointment scheduling / calendar sync

## Tracking / advertising
The current app does not contain advertising SDKs or cross-app advertising tracking.

## Account deletion
Users can initiate account deletion inside the app or at:
https://propertycare365.co.uk/delete-account/

Requests are processed within 30 days. Data that is no longer required is deleted or anonymised. Certain accounting, transaction, service-safety, fraud-prevention or legal records may be retained where required or permitted by law.
