Common questions

Is this insurance?

No. Property Care 365 is a maintenance/service membership. It does not reimburse financial loss or provide cash benefits.

Are parts included?

No. Parts, components, consumables and materials are charged separately. Significant chargeable work is explained before proceeding where reasonably practicable.

Does booking mean the appointment is instantly confirmed?

Not yet. The requested time is checked against the engineer's live calendar and is confirmed once the calendar sync completes.

Can I add LPG properties?

Yes, where the appliance and installation are within the engineer's qualifications and normal scope of work.
