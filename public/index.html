Common questions

Is this insurance?

No. Property Care 360 is a maintenance/service membership. It does not reimburse financial loss or provide cash benefits.

Are parts included?

No. Parts, components, consumables and materials are charged separately. Significant chargeable work is explained before proceeding where reasonably practicable.

Does booking mean the appointment is instantly confirmed?

Not yet. The requested time is checked against the engineer's live calendar and is confirmed once the calendar sync completes.

What heating and hot-water systems can I add?

You can add gas, LPG and oil-heated properties, plus common hot-water arrangements including unvented, vented and combi systems, where the work is within the engineer's qualifications and normal scope.
